# ActiQuest Privacy Policy

**ActiQuest beta web portal and Android application · Version 1.2.0-beta**

---

## 1. Information Handled During the Beta

Depending on the features you use, ActiQuest may process:
- Your email address and Firebase account identifier (`uid`).
- Display name, college, degree program, and section affiliation.
- Quest activity, points earned, active streak counts, and badge achievements.
- Accessibility preference (`adaptiveMode`).
- Consent choices and timestamps.
- Beta security and audit logs (timestamps, account identifiers, event type, endpoint, and anomaly flags).

*The app does not collect or request your date of birth.*

---

## 2. Motion Proofs & Local Processing

Motion proofs are processed locally on-device where supported and minimized to what is required to verify physical quest completion. The web portal does not request camera, microphone, or location permissions. If you choose to submit video or screenshot evidence, only provide the minimum material needed.

---

## 3. Purposes & Lawful Handling (RA 10173 & NPC Circular 2023-04)

Information supports authentication, approved beta access, policy-consent records, account preferences, quest administration, college/department standings, user-requested data exports/deletion, and service security. All processing adheres to the **Proportionality Principle** under Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and NPC Circular 2023-04. 

*Public leaderboard standings display Academic College names and aggregate totals only.*

---

## 4. Storage & Service Providers

The app uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore through the project's configured Firebase infrastructure. Vercel hosts companion web portal API routes. These providers process data under their own privacy commitments.

---

## 5. Retention & Data Subject Rights

Beta test data will be wiped or anonymized when the beta is completed. Under RA 10173, you have the right to request:
- Access to your personal data.
- Correction of inaccurate data.
- Objection to processing.
- Erasure or blocking of records.
- Data portability (JSON export available in Account Settings).

To exercise your rights or request account deletion, use the in-app **Account & Data Management** screen or contact `actiquest.admin@gmail.com`.

---

## 6. Age & Guardian Consent

Beta self-activation is strictly limited to individuals aged **18 or older**. Users under 18 cannot activate an account through self-service; a parent or legal guardian must contact the project team through the user's school, and access remains blocked until independent guardian verification is completed.
